Professional Summary

PhD student at Leiden Observatory studying the evolved phases of stellar evolution (TP-AGB stars) in quiescent galaxies using near-infrared observations. Background in radio and infrared astronomy, with research experience spanning ALMA, JWST and MeerKAT. Enjoys giving talks, popular science writing, and stargazing with a Dobsonian telescope.
